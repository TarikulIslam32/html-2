Common Inquiries

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

For custom printed cartons, our standard MOQ starts at 100 pieces for 5-ply master cartons and 250 pieces for die-cut mailers.

Can you produce boxes to our custom dimensions?

Yes! We manufacture custom die-cut and regular slotted cartons (RSC) to your exact Length, Width, and Height specifications.

We deliver unprinted sample boxes within 24–48 hours for dimensional verification before commencing bulk production.
